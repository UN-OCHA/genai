## Highlights
- Over 9 million children are out of school due to conflict, violence, natural disasters, and displacement.
- More than 6,000 schools closed and approximately 10,000 schools damaged due to climate shocks and conflict.
- Over 9 million malaria cases reported, with 1,267 deaths, primarily in Oromia, Amhara, South Ethiopia, and Southwest regions.
- UNICEF provided lifesaving treatment to over 107,000 acutely malnourished children under five.
- Access to safe drinking water provided to over 571,000 people.
- UNICEF requires US$ 535 million for humanitarian assistance; US$127 million available, leaving a 76% funding gap.

## Funding Overview and Partnerships
- UNICEF's 2024 Humanitarian Action for Children (HAC) Appeal requires US$535.3 million.
- US$95 million received towards the appeal, with US$32.1 million carry-over from 2023.
- Gratitude expressed to donors including CERF, Denmark, UK Aid, US BHA, Japan, Canada, Ireland, Norway, Germany, Sweden, Denmark, and private sector contributions.

## Situation Overview and Humanitarian Needs
- Ongoing conflict in Amhara and Oromia impacting public health.
- Cholera outbreak active in 18 woredas across six regions.
- Malaria epidemic with over 300,000 cases weekly.
- Flooding in Shashego and Gelana woredas affecting thousands.
- Education sector devastated with 9 million children out of school.

## Summary Analysis of Programme Response
### Health
- 713,798 women and children accessed primary health care services.
- Over 30,000 children received measles vaccinations.
- 310,000 individuals received cholera vaccines.

### Nutrition
- Lifesaving nutrition services provided to over 107,000 children.
- 729,000 children received vitamin A supplementation.
- SAM admissions increased in Tigray, Amhara, and other regions.

### WASH
- Over 571,000 people accessed safe drinking water.
- 44,800 people gained access to sanitation facilities.
- 334,000 people reached with handwashing activities.

### Child Protection
- 72,640 children and 34,947 adults reached through child protection and GBV interventions.
- 19,259 individuals received MHPSS services.

### Education
- Nearly 48,000 children accessed education services.
- Over 103,000 children received learning materials.

### Social Protection
- 2,923 conflict-affected households received cash transfers.
- 3,237 individuals linked to various services in Amhara.

### Social and Behavioural Change
- Over 17 million people reached through outreach and mobilization efforts.
- Feedback collected from over 13,000 people.

### Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse
- Over 39,000 individuals reached with information on SEA reporting channels.

## Humanitarian Leadership, Coordination and Strategy
- UNICEF's strategy aligned with the 2024 HRP.
- Active participation in OCHA-led coordination mechanisms.
- Leadership in Nutrition, WASH Clusters, and Child Protection Area of Responsibility.

# Concise Summary
The UNICEF Ethiopia Humanitarian Situation Report highlights the severe impact of conflict, natural disasters, and displacement on education, health, and nutrition in Ethiopia. Over 9 million children are out of school, and more than 9 million malaria cases have been reported. UNICEF has provided lifesaving treatment to over 107,000 acutely malnourished children and safe drinking water to over 571,000 people. The organization requires US$ 535 million for humanitarian assistance, with a current funding gap of 76%. The report details the ongoing efforts in health, nutrition, WASH, child protection, education, social protection, and social and behavioral change, emphasizing the need for continued support and coordination to address the humanitarian needs in Ethiopia.
