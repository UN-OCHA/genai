@UNICEF Ethiopia/2024/ Demissew Bizuwork
October - November 2024

## Highlights
- Over 9 million children are currently out of school across the country due to conflict, violence, natural disasters, and displacement.
- More than 6,000 schools have closed, and approximately 10,000 schools were damaged due to climate shocks and conflict.
- Over 9 million malaria cases, including 1,267 deaths, were reported from Oromia (40.9%), followed by Amhara (23.5%), South Ethiopia (9.1%), and Southwest (7.5%) regions.
- UNICEF provided lifesaving treatment to more than 107,000 acutely malnourished children under five years of age.
- Access to safe drinking water was provided to over 571,000 people.
- UNICEF requires US$ 535 million for humanitarian assistance; US$127 million is available, leaving a 76% funding gap.

## Funding Overview and Partnerships
- UNICEF’s Humanitarian Action for Children (HAC) 2024 Appeal requires US$535.3 million.
- UNICEF Ethiopia has received US$95 million towards the appeal, with US$32.1 million of carry-over from 2023.
- Donors include CERF, Denmark, UK Aid, US BHA, US PRM, Japan, Canada, Ireland, Norway, Germany, Sweden, Denmark, and private sector donors.

## Situation Overview and Humanitarian Needs
- Ongoing conflict in Ethiopia, particularly in Amhara and Oromia regions, impacts public health.
- Cholera outbreak is active in 18 woredas from six regions.
- Malaria has reached epidemic proportions with over 300,000 cases reported weekly.
- Flooding in Shashego woreda affected over 16,000 people.
- Education sector devastated with approximately 9 million children out of school.

## Summary Analysis of Programme Response
### Health
- 713,798 women and children accessed primary health care services.
- Over 30,000 children received measles vaccinations.
- Over 310,000 individuals received cholera vaccines.

### Nutrition
- Lifesaving therapeutic and preventative nutrition services provided to IDPs, refugees, and vulnerable host populations.
- Over 107,000 children received treatment for SAM.

### WASH
- Over 571,000 people accessed safe drinking water.
- 44,800 people gained access to sanitation facilities.

### Child Protection
- 72,640 children and 34,947 adults reached through child protection and GBV interventions.

### Education
- Nearly 48,000 new children reached with formal and non-formal education services.

### Social Protection
- 2,923 conflict-affected households received Shock-Responsive Cash Transfers.

## Humanitarian Leadership, Coordination and Strategy
- UNICEF’s humanitarian strategy is aligned with the 2024 HRP.
- UNICEF leads the Nutrition and WASH Clusters and Child Protection Area of Responsibility (AoR).

# Concise Summary
The UNICEF Ethiopia Humanitarian Situation Report highlights the severe impact of conflict, natural disasters, and displacement on the country's children and public health. Over 9 million children are out of school, and malaria cases have reached epidemic levels. UNICEF's response includes providing healthcare, nutrition, WASH services, and education to affected populations. Despite these efforts, a significant funding gap remains, with only 24% of the required US$535 million secured. The report underscores the urgent need for continued support to address the humanitarian crisis in Ethiopia.
