# UNICEF Ethiopia Humanitarian Situation Report No. 9

## Highlights
- Over 9 million children are currently out of school due to conflict, violence, natural disasters, and displacement.
- More than 6,000 schools have closed, and approximately 10,000 schools were damaged due to climate shocks and conflict.
- Over 9 million malaria cases, including 1,267 deaths, were reported from Oromia, Amhara, South Ethiopia, and Southwest regions.
- UNICEF provided lifesaving treatment to more than 107,000 acutely malnourished children under five years of age.
- Access to safe drinking water was provided to over 571,000 people.
- UNICEF requires US$ 535 million for humanitarian assistance, with a 76% funding gap.

## Funding Overview and Partnerships
- UNICEF’s Humanitarian Action for Children (HAC) 2024 Appeal requires US$535.3 million.
- To date, UNICEF Ethiopia has received US$95 million towards the appeal.
- UNICEF expresses gratitude to donors including CERF, Denmark, UK Aid, US BHA, Japan, Canada, Ireland, Norway, Germany, Sweden, Denmark, and private sector donors.

## Situation Overview and Humanitarian Needs
- Ongoing conflict in Ethiopia, particularly in Amhara and Oromia regions, impacts public health.
- Cholera outbreak active in 18 woredas from six regions.
- Malaria has reached epidemic proportions with over 9 million cases since 2024.
- Heavy rainfall caused flooding in Shashego woreda, affecting over 16,000 people.
- Approximately 9 million children are out of school due to conflict and natural disasters.

## Summary Analysis of Programme Response
### Health
- 713,798 women and children accessed primary health care services.
- Over 30,000 children received measles vaccinations.
- Over 310,000 individuals received cholera vaccines.

### Nutrition
- Lifesaving therapeutic and preventative nutrition services provided to IDPs, refugees, and vulnerable populations.
- Over 107,000 children received treatment for SAM.
- 729,000 children received vitamin A supplementation.

### WASH
- Supported over 571,000 people with access to safe drinking water.
- 44,800 people gained access to sanitation facilities.
- 334,000 people reached with handwashing and behavior change activities.

### Child Protection
- 72,640 children and 34,947 adults reached through child protection and GBV interventions.
- 19,259 individuals received MHPSS services.

### Education
- Nearly 48,000 new children reached with formal and non-formal education services.
- Over 103,000 children received essential learning materials.

### Social Protection
- 2,923 conflict-affected households received Shock-Responsive Cash Transfers.
- Community service workers linked 3,237 individuals to various services.

### Social and Behavioural Change (SBC) and Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP)
- Over 17 million people reached through outreach and mobilization efforts.
- Feedback collected from over 13,000 people regarding services provided.

### Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA)
- Over 39,000 individuals reached with information on safe and accessible SEA reporting channels.

## Humanitarian Leadership, Coordination and Strategy
- UNICEF’s humanitarian strategy is aligned with the 2024 HRP.
- UNICEF leads the Nutrition and WASH Clusters and Child Protection Area of Responsibility (AoR).

## Annex A - Summary of Programme Results
- Nutrition: 965,888 children with severe wasting admitted for treatment.
- Health: 2,876,553 children and women accessed primary healthcare.
- WASH: 6,351,065 people accessed water for drinking and domestic needs.
- Child Protection: 350,000 children, adolescents, and caregivers accessed MHPSS.

## Annex B - 2024 HAC Funding Status
- Total HAC Funding Requirements: US$535,302,121
- Total Funds Available: US$127,747,706
- Funding Gap: 76%
