## Overview
Yemen has faced significant humanitarian challenges in 2024 due to armed conflict and climate change-induced natural disasters. An estimated 531,006 people have been displaced since January 2024, with 93% displaced by climate-related crises and 7% by armed conflict. The United Nations' Rapid Response Mechanism (RRM), led by UNFPA in collaboration with WFP, UNICEF, and other partners, has been crucial in providing immediate assistance. From January to December 2024, the RRM reached 463,204 individuals, representing 87% of those registered for assistance across 21 governorates. Vulnerable groups included 22% female-headed households, 21% elderly, and 10% people with disabilities. The RRM also enhances aid delivery coordination through data sharing with various humanitarian organizations.

# Summary
In 2024, Yemen faced severe humanitarian challenges due to armed conflict and climate change-induced disasters, displacing over half a million people. The UN's Rapid Response Mechanism (RRM), led by UNFPA and partners, provided critical aid to 463,204 individuals across 21 governorates, focusing on vulnerable groups. The RRM improved aid coordination through data sharing with humanitarian organizations. Key efforts included winterization support and timely assistance delivery. The report highlights the involvement of numerous organizations and the need for continued support to address water, sanitation, hygiene, education, and protection services.
